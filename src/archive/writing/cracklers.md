And naturally the water is like ice—it leaked from snow to slip down face and scree and wait here so suitably in its basin, like they were made for each other, the lake and the granite. Of course it recalls its pale birth. The same white now laps along filmy rock and makes gooseflesh of bare legs. There is also a shelf and another face. This one is peering over. It is clear all the way down, what did you expect?
