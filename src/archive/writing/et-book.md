:sc[ET Book] is a [webfont](https://edwardtufte.github.io/et-book/) of the typeface used in Edward Tufte’s books, drawn by Dmitry Krasny, Bonnie Scranton, and Tufte himself.

It is also the serif used on this site. I have my own custom [fork](https://github.com/keatonelvins/et-book) with some rendering issues fixed and language support extended and a few other miscellaneous tweaks. Many thanks to [Daphne Preston-Kendal](https://dpk.land) and [Adam Schwartz](https://adamschwartz.co) for doing 99% of the work here!
:::aside[serif]
my sans/mono is Pangram Pangram's [Neue Montreal](https://neuemontreal.com) for those interested
:::

It is very pretty. I especially love the italic looptail _g_ and the fj ligature. 

fjord fjord fjord fjord fjord. _fjord_!
